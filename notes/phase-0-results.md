## Set

The set is synthetic. It stands in for a Marble export and cost nothing. It is generated by `spike/make-sample-set.mjs` (`npm run make-set` in `spike/`), deterministic (seeded), and written to the git-ignored `scenes/` folder. No Marble spend.

The room: 6 m wide (x -3 to 3), 8 m deep (z -4 to 4), 3 m high, floor, four walls and ceiling in distinct muted colours. A red door slab on the back wall (z = -4): 0.90 m wide, 2.00 m tall, 0.05 m proud, centred at x = 1.0. A blue chair-seat box 0.45 m by 0.45 m by 0.45 m high at x = -1.5, z = -2. A letter F sign on the back wall, 0.6 m tall, bottom at y = 1.8, centred at x = -1.5.

The files are stored like an assumed Marble export: OpenCV axes (y down, z forward) and raw units. raw = (x, -(y - 1.2), -z) / 2.5. So the raw floor is at y = 0.48, the door is 0.8 raw units tall, and the correct scale factor is 2.5. The ground truth, in metric and raw, is in `scenes/set-01-truth.json`.

One run each on SPZ, Playwright Chromium: 2M SPZ read 34 ms, parse 310 ms, first frame 442 ms, 60 fps. 500k SPZ read 27 ms, parse 94 ms, first frame 175 ms, 60 fps. Single runs, not the three the brief asks for.

Limits of these numbers:
- 60 fps is the display refresh cap in headless mode. The runs show the 2M set holds 60 fps. How far above 60 it could go is unmeasured.
- The first run of a pair is the slowest. The files are on local disk, so read time is mostly cache state, and a real download over a network will be slower. Unmeasured.
- The synthetic room is thin surfaces only, with no hidden depth, so it is probably easier to render than a real scan. Unmeasured on a real export.
- The Claude Browser pane was tried first and not used: it pauses animation frames while the pane is hidden, so one run there read 1.9 fps and a 141 s first frame. That is the pane, not the renderer, and the run is excluded.

Proposed thresholds, for the owner to confirm: first frame under 15 seconds, 30 fps or more at 2M splats. On this set both are met by a wide margin in both engines, so the 500k fallback is not needed. This says nothing yet about a real Marble export.

## Format, PLY and SPZ

PLY is what was tested for the three-run table. Spark 2.4.0 loads both PLY and SPZ with no trouble: the single SPZ runs above loaded and passed the same axes checks. SPZ is about 12 to 13 times smaller than the PLY here (8.4 MB against 112 MB at 2M). SPZ quantisation moves the ceiling by 0.0005 raw units (0.7197 against 0.7202). Visual effect not judged.

## Splat defects (brief step 6)

Holes, floaters, smeared areas and what happens behind objects cannot be judged on a synthetic set. They stay unmeasured until a real Marble export is loaded.

## Axes

Result: floor below, text readable. Screenshot: `reference/phase-0-set-01-start-pose.png` (git-ignored, Playwright Chromium, 1280 by 720, start pose, facing the back wall).

The fix: Spark's splat is rotated 180 degrees about x (`mesh.rotation.x = Math.PI`), which flips y and z and turns the OpenCV frame into three.js axes. The rotation was right the first time, so no further fix was needed. After the rotation the scene is still in raw units (scale is Task 4), and the raw floor lands at y = -0.48 world.

Checked by number, on the data after the rotation (`axesReport` in `spike/main.js`, shown on the page and in `window.__results.axes`):
- Bounding box of splat centres: x -1.2002 to 1.2002, y -0.48 to 0.7202, z -1.5996 to 1.5996. That is the room divided by 2.5, with the floor at -0.48 (metric 0 minus 1.2, divided by 2.5) and the ceiling at 0.72. Matches the truth file.
- Walk camera start (0, 0.16, 0.8): inside the box, floor below it (-0.48 < 0.16), ceiling above it. In metric that is eye height 1.6 m, 2.0 m from the room centre, facing the back wall (-z).
- F not mirrored: the near-black splats (the F) are projected through a camera at the start pose. The stem sits left of the top bar (NDC x -0.4437 against -0.4116 mean), the top bar is above the stem bottom (NDC y 0.287 against 0.072), and the bar reaches right of the stem. Stated as `notMirrored: true` in all 14 runs.
- By eye: the screenshot shows an F with stem on the left and bars to the right, the red door on the right of the back wall, the blue seat on the left near the floor. Same layout as the truth file.

The SPZ files give the same box and the same F result.

Not checked yet: Chrome and Safari proper (pending controller run), and the collider GLB has not been loaded in the viewer.
